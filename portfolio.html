Data Project

Is the AI revolution having an impact on economic indicators? Do these effects differ across regions or for different levels of development?

This project explores the impact of artificial intelligence on global economic indicators and analyzes whether these benefits differ across geographic regions and levels of economic development. During the research process, I used Stata to construct regression models, classify and conduct empirical analyses on cross-country data, and apply economic methods to conduct hypothesis testing and derive results from the data. The research findings analyze the impact of AI technology on productivity and the economy of countries at different stages of development, demonstrating my ability to use statistical models to address complex macroeconomic issues and conduct data analysis.
